Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the DOPE RUST Discord application (the "Bot") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The Bot is operated by the DOPE RUST team to manage our Discord community for the DOPE RUST Rust game-server network.

The Bot is powered by MEE6 (Bot Personalizer / custom bot feature). MEE6 provides the underlying software and processes data on our behalf. This policy covers our use of the Bot; MEE6's and Discord's own handling of data is governed by their respective policies, linked below.

Who we are

The data controller for the Bot is the operator of the DOPE RUST Discord server. For any privacy request or question, contact us at [YOUR-CONTACT-EMAIL].

What data we collect

The Bot only processes data from the Discord server(s) it is added to. Depending on the features in use, this includes:

Account & member data

  • Discord user ID, username, display name, and avatar
  • Server roles, join and leave events, and membership changes (such as boosts)
  • Profile changes visible within the server (for example, nickname or avatar updates)

Message content

  • The content of messages sent in channels the Bot can access, including edits and deletions
  • This is used for automated moderation and for audit logging in staff-only channels

Activity (presence) data

  • Online status and activity information exposed by Discord, used by the MEE6 platform for engagement and activity features

Data you provide directly

  • Information you submit through Bot commands or interactions (for example, when using a command or reacting to a message)

How we use your data

  • Onboarding: welcoming new members and assigning roles automatically
  • Moderation: filtering spam and prohibited content, and enforcing server rules
  • Audit logging: recording member, role, and message events in staff channels for accountability and safety
  • Community features: leveling, engagement, and other MEE6 features enabled on the server

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or process it for any purpose beyond running this community.

Legal basis (GDPR)

For members in the EU/EEA and UK, we process this data on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating, moderating, and securing our community (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where required, processing also relies on your consent, given by choosing to join and participate in the server. You may object to processing at any time by leaving the server or contacting us.

Third parties & data sharing

The Bot runs on infrastructure provided by MEE6 and Discord. Data necessary to operate the Bot is processed by these providers:

  • MEE6 — see the MEE6 Privacy Policy
  • Discord — see the Discord Privacy Policy

We do not share your data with any other third party except where required by law.

Data retention

Audit & moderation logsKept only as long as needed for community safety and accountability, then removed.
Member & activity dataRetained while you are a member of the server and processed live; not stored beyond what the features require.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Request a copy of your data (portability)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [YOUR-CONTACT-EMAIL]. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

Discord requires all users to be at least 13 years old (or older where local law requires). The Bot is not intended for anyone below this age, and we do not knowingly process data from them.

Changes to this policy
